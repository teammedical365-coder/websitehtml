Billing & Claims FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions on Hospital RCM

Everything you need to know about cashless insurance processing, package pricing, and revenue leakage control.

How does Medical365 streamline TPA and cashless insurance processing?

+
Medical365 features a dedicated TPA Insurance Desk that manages digital pre-authorizations, query resolutions, interim approvals, and final discharge summaries for Star Health, Medi Assist, ICICI Lombard, Vidal Health, Paramount, and all major Indian insurers. It reduces cashless claim approval wait times from 6 hours to under 45 minutes.

Is the billing module integrated with the National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX)?

+
Yes. Medical365 is designed with native connectors for the National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX) protocol initiated by NHA and IRDAI, facilitating real-time machine-to-machine cashless claim adjudications with standardized FHIR claim profiles.

How does the system prevent hospital revenue leakage?

+
Medical365 automatically cross-verifies physician medication orders, OT consumables, nursing procedures, and diagnostic investigations against the final inpatient bill. Unbilled items trigger real-time alerts before a discharge summary can be generated, eliminating unbilled service revenue leakage by up to 98%.

Does Medical365 support complex surgical package pricing and split billing?

+
Yes. You can configure multi-tier surgical packages (e.g., Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy, Total Knee Replacement) that bundle room rent, doctor fees, OT charges, and consumable allowances. If a patient exceeds package limits, the software intelligently bifurcates extra charges into co-pay or out-of-pocket bills.

Is the billing software compliant with Indian GST laws and accounting exports?

+
Yes. Every pharmacy invoice, inpatient room charge, and corporate billing statement includes appropriate HSN/SAC codes and B2B/B2C GST calculations. Data exports smoothly into Tally, SAP, Marg, and QuickBooks.
