Diagnostic Questions

Frequently Asked Questions on Medical365 LIMS

Everything you need to know about analyzer connectivity, NABL quality control, and report distribution.

What is bidirectional analyzer interfacing in Medical365 LIMS?

+
Bidirectional interfacing allows the LIMS to transmit test worklists directly to medical lab analyzers (via ASTM/HL7 protocols) and automatically fetch analyzed results without manual data entry. This eliminates transcription typos, accelerates report generation by 80%, and guarantees absolute clinical data integrity.

Which clinical analyzer brands are compatible with Medical365 LIMS?

+
Medical365 interfaces with over 150 leading clinical chemistry, hematology, immunology, and coagulation analyzers including Roche (Cobas), Abbott (Architect), Mindray, Sysmex, Beckman Coulter, Siemens, Bio-Rad, Horiba, Erba, and Transasia.

How does Medical365 support NABL accreditation compliance?

+
The software includes automated Levey-Jennings (LJ) quality control charts, Westgard multi-rule violation alerts, equipment calibration logs, sample rejection tracking, turnaround time (TAT) monitoring, and tamper-proof electronic pathologist authorization.

Can patients download diagnostic reports via WhatsApp and QR codes?

+
Yes. Upon pathologist verification, a secure PDF with the lab's accredited header, digital signature, and verification QR code is immediately sent via WhatsApp and SMS to the patient and referring doctor.

Does the system manage B2B collection centers and doctor commissions?

+
Yes. Medical365 features a complete multi-tier commercial management suite for B2B collection franchises, sample batch tracking, customized price lists, wallet balances, referral accounting, and GST-compliant invoicing.
