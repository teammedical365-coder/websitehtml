Frequently Asked Questions on ABDM HIMS

Everything you need to know about ABHA integration, digital health incentives, and compliance.

What is ABDM compliance in hospital management software?

+
ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) compliance means that hospital software adheres to National Health Authority (NHA) standards. It enables seamless integration with the ABHA system for patient identification, Health Facility Registry (HFR), Health Professional Registry (HPR), and standardized FHIR R4 interoperable data exchange across India.

What are ABDM Milestones M1, M2, and M3?

+
Milestone 1 (M1) covers ABHA ID creation, Aadhaar/mobile verification, and QR code scan-and-share for zero-wait OPD registration. Milestone 2 (M2) designates the hospital as a Health Information Provider (HIP) to securely share diagnostic and discharge records. Milestone 3 (M3) designates the hospital as a Health Information User (HIU), enabling doctors to view longitudinal patient medical history with patient digital consent.

How does Medical365 help hospitals claim ABDM DHIS financial incentives?

+
The National Health Authority offers the Digital Health Incentive Scheme (DHIS), paying hospitals financial incentives (up to ₹4 crore) for transactions linked to ABHA. Medical365 automatically registers eligible ABHA OPD/IPD events and submits compliant transaction logs to the NHA portal so your hospital maximizes incentive payouts without manual bookkeeping.

Is Medical365 compliant with DPDP Act 2023 and healthcare data privacy?

+
Yes. Medical365 is fully compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. All patient records, prescriptions, and diagnostic reports are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), hosted on secure Indian cloud infrastructure with granular, consent-driven role-based access control.

How quickly can our hospital integrate ABDM with Medical365?

+
Because Medical365 has pre-certified NHA sandbox connectors, our team can migrate your existing facility and operationalize ABDM M1, M2, and M3 workflows within 3 to 7 business days, including HFR and HPR registry onboarding.
