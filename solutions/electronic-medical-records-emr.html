Clinical Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions on Medical365 AI EMR

Everything you need to know about AI dictation, specialty templates, and clinical safety.

How does Medical365's AI clinical scribe save doctor consultation time?

+
Medical365's integrated AI Clinical Scribe listens to ambient doctor-patient consultations and converts conversational speech into structured clinical SOAP notes (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) with 99.2% clinical accuracy. Doctors spend up to 70% less time typing on keyboards, allowing them to focus entirely on direct patient care.

Does Medical365 EMR support specialty-specific clinical templates?

+
Yes. Medical365 features over 40 pre-configured clinical templates designed for specific medical branches including Cardiology, Pediatrics (with WHO growth charts and immunization tracking), OB-GYN (antenatal care and delivery records), Orthopedics, Ophthalmology (visual acuity and IOP charts), Dermatology, and Oncology.

How does the system prevent medication errors and adverse drug reactions?

+
Our smart e-prescribing module features a real-time clinical decision support system (CDSS) that alerts physicians to severe drug-drug interactions, duplicate therapies, dosage anomalies, and registered patient drug/food allergies before a prescription is finalized.

Can patients receive their digital prescriptions via WhatsApp and SMS?

+
Yes. As soon as a doctor signs the e-prescription, an official branded PDF with hospital letterhead, digital signature, and secure QR code is automatically dispatched to the patient's WhatsApp and registered phone number, eliminating illegible paper slips.

Is Medical365 EMR compliant with NABH and ABDM digital standards?

+
Yes. Medical365 complies with all NABH digital record documentation benchmarks (audit trails, time stamps, version history, non-erasable doctor notes) and is certified for National Health Authority ABDM M1, M2, and M3 data exchange.
